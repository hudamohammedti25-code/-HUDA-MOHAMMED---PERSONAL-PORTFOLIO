A LITTLE INTRODUCTION

About Me

With Yemeni and Indonesian roots, and a soul deeply rooted in Saudi Arabia, I am a girl who dares to do the impossible. I let my imagination soar among the clouds of peace and optimism, believing that dreams have no borders and possibilities have no limits. I am an Informatics Engineering student based in Indonesia, passionate about creativity, technology, programming, and digital art. Alongside my academic journey, I serve as a leader and media supervisor at the Sawa'ed Volunteer Team, where I contribute to creative media work and initiatives that promote volunteerism and positive community impact. I aspire to transform my ideas into meaningful achievements, continuously develop my skills, and leave a unique and lasting mark on people across different countries, cultures, and corners of the world.
